# Roadmap
- V0.1: initial browser deployment.
- V0.2: full 9 km elevation range, Everest preset, anti-aliased solid water, selectable terrain detail. Browser validation pending.
- V0.3: stable independently tiled inundation elevation raster, ocean connectivity and coastal validation; avoid reliance on rendered mesh LOD for classification.
- V0.4: land-area and population exposure summaries, geocoding, public deployment optimization.
- Future: negative sea levels, bathymetry, historical scenarios and regional high-resolution data.
